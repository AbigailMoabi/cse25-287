Abigail Moabi

Founder & Lead Developer

My name is ABIGAIL MOABI. My journey in higher education has been full of challenges and growth. Imagine being away from home to study, trying to balance your independence, and overcoming self-doubt.

All these experiences shaped me. I realized that there are some students like me who thrive when they connect in their own way—especially when they can share knowledge without the pressure of a crowd.

"That is why I created Study With Me... A global digital space where students across the world can come together, learn, and grow united."
